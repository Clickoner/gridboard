Gridboard

A free planner for your Instagram grid.

Drop your pictures in, drag them into the order you want, keep a few layouts to compare, mark what is already posted, and save the grid as an image when you are happy.

Your pictures never leave this browser. There is no account, nothing is uploaded, and nothing to pay. The board is kept in this browser's own storage, so it is here when you come back on this device. Clearing the browser's site data clears the board, so save the grid image for anything you want to keep.

Tiles are 4:5, the shape Instagram's profile grid has shown since 2025. 1:1 is there for the old look. On a phone, hold a tile for a moment before dragging it.

Gridboard is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta. Made in Australia.

On every device

Turn on sync and this board gets a private link. Open the link on your phone and the same board is there, pictures included. Anyone holding the link can edit the board, so keep it to yourself.
